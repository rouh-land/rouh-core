# Structure

One page with a title page beside it (desk) or over it (phone). From the journeys, top to bottom:

1. **Head**: the headline sentence that counts the list shown ("1 watching, 7 to watch, 2 watched."), and Add a title as a field-shaped button (it reads like the box you type in). Not a filled button: the one primary press of the page is Tonight's.
2. **Lists strip** (owner only): My list, each list, All lists, each with its count, in one row that scrolls sideways inside itself with a fade at the edge when there are more; New list at its end. Shown always, so New list is always one press away (before, it was a top button beside two others).
3. **Who sees it**: one line under the strip for the list shown: "Only you see My list" with Share a list; on a list of one's own "Only you" with Share this list and List settings; on a shared list the faces with "joined" or "invited" and People and invites. On the other person's page: "Shared by Petar" and Leave this list.
4. **Tonight**: the pick, big: poster, title, next episode, length and service, one sentence why; the primary press; Where to watch; Something else; the time they have as a small segmented choice; After that as three small rows. On a phone the poster sits beside the title and the presses go full width.
5. **The shelf**: Up next, Watched, All as one segmented choice with counts; the search field; Filter (with how many are on) which opens Kind, Where, Tag and Sort in one row; each filter that is on shown as a chip with a cross; the posters.
6. **One title**: a panel on a desk (beside the list), the whole screen on a phone. Order: Back; poster, title and facts; the one next press with Where to watch beside it; the history line; status; episodes; My rating (the pad); Tags; Notes (recommended by, where, notes, seen before); Links; About; More (Move to, Edit the details, Look it up again, Remove from the list).
7. **Sheets**: New list; Share a list; People and invites with the list's settings (rename, stop sharing, delete).

Old home -> new home:
- The three top buttons (Add a title, New list, Share a list) -> Add a title in the head; New list at the end of the lists strip; Share a list on the "who sees it" line.
- The list pills that wrapped into rows on a phone -> one row that scrolls inside itself.
- Four labelled dropdowns always shown -> Filter, which opens them; chips for the ones on.
- The title page's long single column -> grouped sections, the next press and Where to watch first.
Must-keep -> where: every item of brief.md is on the views above; nothing removed.
