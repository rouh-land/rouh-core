The awkward real states, each from today's data or the code's own cases (`rouh/apps_hosting.py`, `page`).

1. **Today's real state:** 6 apps all answering, on 2 servers that are not apps servers (5 and 1), the second one's data unknown to Rouh, 4 pages with data on Rouh, a plan of 10, none moved, no apps server. (`a-today`) Journeys 1, 4, 5, 8.
2. **An app not answering, and a failed backup**, on a ready apps server, plus one on the sites server: the verdict turns, Needs you has two lines with their fixes. (`a-down`) Journeys 1, 2.
3. **Opened from a notice:** one app on its own, not answering, the fix first. (`a-app`, the down app) Journey 2.
4. **A command on its way:** Update sent, waiting for the server, then running: a live line, the other commands held until it finishes. (`a-app`) Journey 3.
5. **Nothing at all:** first visit, no app, no server. (`a-empty`) Journey 6.
6. **Waiting for a new server to call in:** setup line made, the live wait. (`a-setup`) Journey 4.
7. **A silent apps server:** called in last 3 h ago. (`a-down`, in Needs you) Journey 2.
8. **Long names and addresses on a phone:** "Wedding invitations for Ana and Boris", `https://invitations.ana-and-boris.example.com` wrap or shorten, never cut or push sideways. (`a-down`) Journeys 1, 3.
