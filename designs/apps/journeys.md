# Journeys: Apps

Written first, before any page (28 November 2026). The owner's own list is the spine, in his order: is everything up, what needs me, set up a server, open or change one app, what lives on Rouh and the moving plan. Three more are drawn from the feature's request and today's data.
The two most frequent are marked **(most frequent)**.

## 1. Is everything up? (most frequent)
- **Starts:** Work, Apps, Dashboard in the rail, on a desk or a phone.
- **In his head:** "Are all my apps working?"
- **Answer:** one sentence: how many apps answer, how many do not, when they were last checked. A state shape on every app.
- **Action:** none, or Check now.
- **Target:** the answer is the first line of the first screen, 0 presses after opening, read in 3 s, at 1440 and at 390 px.

## 2. What needs me? (most frequent)
- **Starts:** a phone notice ("Meeting notes is not answering") that opens Apps, or the Dashboard itself.
- **In his head:** "What is broken, and what do I press?"
- **Answer:** a Needs you list right under the verdict, each line saying what happened and since when, with its fix beside it. Nothing to do: one quiet line that says so.
- **Action:** Restart, Roll back, Logs or Ask Rouh to fix it (a down app); Back up now (a failed backup); Ask for a report (a silent server).
- **Target:** visible without scrolling; the fix is 1 press from the Dashboard, and 1 press from the notice, which opens that app on its own.

## 3. Open or change one app
- **Starts:** the Dashboard.
- **In his head:** "Take me to my watch list" or "I want the wedding page to also ask for allergies."
- **Answer:** the app's row, with its address.
- **Action:** Open it (its address, 1 press on the row's address). Change it: open the app (1 press), Ask Rouh to change it (1 press), type. Update, Restart, Roll back, Logs, Back up now: open the app, then 1 press.
- **Target:** Open in 1 press; a change in 2 presses plus typing; every command in 2 presses, and its progress shows by itself.

## 4. Set up my apps server
- **Starts:** the Dashboard, where it is the next step while there is none.
- **In his head:** "Get me the server the apps should live on, with as little of my time as possible."
- **Answer:** which host, what to buy, what to paste, and that it turns Ready by itself.
- **Action:** Set up an apps server (1), Hostinger already picked, Make my setup line (2), Copy (3); then the host's own buying steps; Ready shows by itself. Or, with a Hostinger token: Set it up on a bought VPS with 1 press.
- **Target:** 3 presses in Rouh plus the host's own steps; the waiting state is visible and live.

## 5. What lives where, and the moving plan
- **Starts:** the Dashboard.
- **In his head:** "What still runs on Rouh or on the old servers, and what is the plan to move it?"
- **Answer:** every app and every page with its own data, grouped by where it runs, with a count per place, and one line: how many are to move and how many have moved.
- **Action:** see the plan (1 press); Ask Rouh to move one (once the apps server is ready).
- **Target:** where everything lives is on the Dashboard, 0 presses; the plan in 1 press; nothing moves until he says.

## 6. Build a new app
- **Starts:** the Dashboard, or the first visit with nothing yet.
- **In his head:** "Can Rouh make me an app for this?"
- **Answer:** on a first visit, what Apps is and why, in 5 s; later, a Build a new app button always in the head.
- **Action:** Build a new app, or an idea, puts the sentence in the message box.
- **Target:** 1 press to start asking; understood in 5 s on the first visit.

## 7. Bring in an app that already runs somewhere
- **Starts:** the Dashboard.
- **In his head:** "Watch this app too."
- **Action:** Add an app you already have (1), name, address, server or where (3 fields), Add (2).
- **Target:** 2 presses and 3 fields; it is checked within five minutes.

## 8. Are my apps backed up?
- **Starts:** the Dashboard.
- **In his head:** "If the server dies, do I lose anything?"
- **Answer:** on an apps server, each app's last backup and test restore on its row; elsewhere, said once for the whole place ("Not backed up by Rouh"), not on every row.
- **Target:** 0 presses.
